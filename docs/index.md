  - title: Real pack odds
    details: 11 cards a pack, 1 rare, 3 uncommons and 7 commons, with a holo about every third pack. No card appears twice in a pack.
  - title: An opening worth watching
    details: Tear the pack, swipe the cards away one by one and get a burst of light when the rare is a holo.
  - title: Cobblemon on every card
    details: Card art and pack mascots are drawn from Cobblemon's models, posed like its Pokédex.
  - title: Earn packs by playing
    details: First catches, shiny catches, level milestones and Pokédex progress give packs. The Card Dealer villager sells them too.
  - title: Datapack driven
    details: Sets, cards and reward rules are plain JSON. Add a set or change the odds without touching code.
