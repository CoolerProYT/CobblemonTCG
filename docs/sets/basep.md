# Wizards Black Star Promos

The promo cards Wizards of the Coast gave out from 1999 to 2003 at events, in magazines, movie theaters and toy boxes: **53 cards**, set id `cobblemontcg:basep`. Sold by Card Dealers from the Journeyman level, and given for catching Pokémon that only appear in the promos (Mew, Dragonite, Moltres, Articuno, Psyduck and the Johto Pokémon like Pichu, Togepi, Scizor, Entei, Suicune, Celebi and Ho-Oh).

## Pack odds

<PackOdds set="basep" />

The real promos were never sold in booster packs and all share the Promo rarity. So they fit in a pack, the mod gives each one a regular rarity: Venusaur, Dragonite and most legendary and mythical Pokémon are holo rares, the special Pikachu, Dark Persian, Scizor, Machamp and Snorlax are rares, the Trainers and the rest are uncommons and commons.

Scizor is a Metal Pokémon, the only Metal card in the mod so far.
