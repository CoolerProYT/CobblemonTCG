# Team Rocket

The April 2000 expansion with the Dark Pokémon: **83 cards**, set id `cobblemontcg:base5`. Sold by Card Dealers at the Master level. Its Pokémon all appeared in earlier sets, so no catch gives Team Rocket packs.

## Pack odds

Every Team Rocket holo rare (1 to 17) also exists as a non-holo rare: the Dark Pokémon at 18 to 31, the two Trainers at 71 and 72 and Rainbow Energy at 80. Dark Raichu (83) is the secret holo rare: the real card is numbered 83/82.
