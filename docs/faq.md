# FAQ

## The game does not start

Cobblemon: TCG needs **Cobblemon 1.8.1 or newer** for Minecraft 1.21.1. Check that Cobblemon is installed, and Fabric API on Fabric.

## My cards show drawn art, not Cobblemon models

The models are drawn a few cards per tick after you join a world, so give it a moment after joining or after `F3 + T`. Pokémon Cobblemon has not added yet keep the drawn art.

## I stopped getting packs from catching Pokémon

Check, in order:

1. A first catch only has a **10% chance** of a pack, and each species only gets that one roll.
2. You may have hit the **daily limit** of 10 reward packs (resets at midnight UTC).
3. The server may have turned rewards off; see [Configuration](./guide/configuration#rewards).

Shiny catches always give a pack while you are under the daily limit.

## My villager will not become a Card Dealer

The villager has to be unemployed and not a nitwit, and needs to reach the Card Dealer Table. A villager that has already traded keeps its profession. See [Card Dealer](./guide/card-dealer).

## The Card Dealer does not sell Jungle packs

Jungle unlocks at the **Apprentice** level. Buy about 5 packs from the dealer to level it up. Servers can change the order with `shop.sets`.

## Can I get a holo print of any card?

Packs only give holo prints of Rare Holo cards. Operators can give any card's holo print with `/tcg give card <set> <number> true`.

## Can I trade cards with other players?

Cards are normal items: drop them, put them in chests or trade them however you like. Holo and regular prints of the same card are different items and do not stack.
