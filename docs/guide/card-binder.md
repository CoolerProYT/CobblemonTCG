# Card Binder

A binder keeps your collection out of your inventory: **24 pages of 9 pockets**, 216 pockets in all. Each pocket holds a stack of up to 64 copies of one card.

<RecipeCard id="card_binder" />

## Using it

Right-click the binder to open it. You see two pages (a spread) at a time, with every card shown big in its pocket.

- **Add cards** by placing them in a pocket, or shift-click cards in your inventory: they go onto matching cards first, then into the first free pocket from the open spread on.
- **Turn pages** with the arrows, the scroll wheel over the pages, the left and right arrow keys, or Page Up and Page Down.
- **Sort** with the button in the corner. It merges duplicates and orders the whole binder by set, card number and holo, starting from the first pocket.

Only cards fit in the pockets; the binder cannot hold packs or another binder. Its tooltip shows how many cards it holds and how many pockets are in use.

::: warning
The cards live inside the binder item. If the binder burns in lava or fire, its cards drop out where it was destroyed, like a shulker box's contents.
:::
