## 1.1.0

### New set: Wizards Black Star Promos
- 53 promo cards (set id `cobblemontcg:basep`), each with a regular and a holo print, showing Cobblemon's models like the other sets. Entei, Celebi and Suicune use drawn art until Cobblemon adds their models.
- Three pack wrappers: Pikachu, Mew and Ho-Oh.
- Its own card style: slate art window rim, silver Pokédex strip, starlight over the scenery and a black star set mark.
- The real promos were never sold in packs, so each card has a regular rarity to fit the usual 11 card pack (1 rare, 3 uncommons, 7 commons).

### Other changes
- Card Dealers sell Black Star Promos packs from the Journeyman level.
- Catching a Pokémon that only appears in the promos (Mew, Dragonite, Moltres, Articuno, Psyduck and the Johto Pokémon) gives a Black Star Promos pack instead of a Base Set pack.
- New Metal type (Scizor).
- Trainer cards show their kind (Stadium, Rocket's Secret Machine) above their rules.

### Note for existing configs
`shop.sets` in `config/cobblemontcg-common.toml` is not changed for you. Add `"cobblemontcg:basep"` to it to have Card Dealers sell the new packs.
